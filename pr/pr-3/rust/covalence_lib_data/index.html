Shared byte buffers and containers.

An internal facade (a crate that re-exports other crates) for Covalence’s own crates. Its API may change without notice.

  • Re-exports [bytes], [hashbrown], [indexmap], [smallvec], [smol_str] and arrayvec, with their main types at the root.
  • Adds the Symbol alias.
  • Bytes::copy_from_slice establishes immutable ownership at an untrusted boundary. Clones and slices share that owned storage.
  • An IndexMap keeps insertion order. That is not canonical CBOR wire ordering.
  • Serialization lives in covalence-lib-serde.
